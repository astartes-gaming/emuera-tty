**emuera-tty** plays Emuera (eraBasic / ERB) games in a terminal. It is a
from-scratch reimplementation of the Emuera engine in Rust, with saves that
move both ways between it and Emuera.exe.

New here? [Install it](Installation), then [choose a game](Choosing-a-Game)
and see [Playing](Playing) for the controls.
